# Bringing Puduthangal Lake back to life 

புதுத்தாங்கல் ஏரி மீண்டு வருகிறது 

The lake on Mudichur Road, Tambaram, lay dry and neglected for 15–16 years. Since 2 October 2026 we have been clearing it and reshaping it, so it can hold rainwater again and keep floodwater away from the homes around it. In October 2026 the water came back.

## A women's trust, working for our water 

Five Falls Women's Welfare Trust ( ஐந்தருவி மகளிர் நல அறக்கட்டளை ) is a registered trust (Regd. No. 87/2018) based in Selaiyur, East Tambaram, Chennai. We work for the welfare of women and for the lakes our neighbourhoods depend on. 

Women from local self-help groups are at the heart of our work. Alongside the big clean-up on 10 October 2026, they held a Pongal event at the lake. 

Founder and Secretary 

### S. Shanthi · எஸ். சாந்தி 

Shanthi has worked with ExNoRa, Chennai's environmental movement, for about 30 years. She leads the lake work in the field, bringing together residents, volunteers and government offices.

## 25 years of work with women and communities 

We design and carry out plans to help women and underprivileged people stand on their own feet. It began with five women in 2000. 

- 2000–2001 

Five young women take the step from housewives to entrepreneurs. In 2001 S. Shanthi forms the Five Falls Women's Welfare Society (Reg. No. 252/2001). 

- 2001–2010 

Self-help groups. We form and manage self-help groups for women across Tamil Nadu. 

- 2010–2018 

Education and relief. Education work in Chengalpattu and Thiruvallur districts, and essentials for people hit by the 2015 Chennai floods and the 2016 Vardah cyclone. 

- 2018 onwards 

The Society becomes the Trust (Regd. No. 87/2018), with self-realisation programmes such as Vaazhvin Uyaram Velvom and Maathi Yosi. 

- COVID-19 

STOP CORONA. We work with the Tamil Nadu Government at Rajiv Gandhi Hospital, and continue with medical camps and awareness programmes. 

- Today 

Water bodies, alongside our work with women. See below.

## The lake project 

Puduthangal Lake · Mudichur Road · Ward 52, Zone 4 · Tambaram 

### The problem 

For 15–16 years the lake held no water. Brush and rubbish filled its bed, and the families around it lost a water source. With nothing to hold the rain, a heavy monsoon could flood nearby homes. 

### What we've done 

An excavator cleared brush and rubbish and reshaped the lake bed and its banks. Phase one is finished, the lake holds water again, and phase two has begun. 

### Done the proper way 

The Water Resources Department gave its permission (NOC) for the work, and Tambaram City Municipal Corporation, Zone 4, supports it. 

### So far 

- 02.10.2026 

Work begins on Gandhi Jayanti with a puja and a JCB. The first phase was finished within a week. 

- 10.10.2026 

Mass clean-up with volunteers and Tambaram Corporation, and a Pongal event at the lake by women from local self-help groups. 

- 13.10.2026 

Reshaping the lake. The excavator clears the bed, levels the ground by the boundary wall and shapes the banks to hold rainwater. 

- 18.10.2026 

The water comes back, after about 15 years. The team holds a thanksgiving puja and Pongal. 

- 23.10.2026 

Tree planting launched by Hon'ble Minister Thiru. D. Sarathkumar, with Corporation Commissioner Tmt. R. Saranya, I.A.S. 

- 05.11.2026 

Lake protection and restoration event. Phase one complete; the Minister joins an awareness walk and plants saplings. 

- Next 

Phase two of the work, and awareness events at schools, colleges and lakes around Tambaram.
